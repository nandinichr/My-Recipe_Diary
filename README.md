# Creative Game Finder

My app allows Steam Game players who are interested in art, interior design, and creativity decide the games they want to buy by comparing their creative features, player ratings, and gameplay value

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Steams has thousands of games which makes it difficult for players interested in art, interior design, and creativity to find games that match their interests while also offering good ratings and value for their money.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Sub-question 1:** What specific games focus on creative aspects such as decorating, art, building, and designing?
2. **Sub-question 2:** Which creative games consist of the highest ratings?
3. **Sub-question 3:** What creative games offer the longest amount of game play for the price they’re worth?

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
