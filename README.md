# My Recipe Diary

My app helps anyone decide what recipes they can make by taking pictures or entering the groceries or food that already exist in their fridge or pantries.

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
College students and busy home cooks often open the fridge, see a random mix of ingredients, and default to takeout or let food go to waste. Deciding what to make from what’s on hand means mentally matching ingredients to recipes, which is slow and easy to give up on. My app does that matching automatically.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Sub-question 1:** What recipes can users make with the ingredients they currently have?
2. **Sub-question 2:** For near-matches, which ingredients would I need to buy?
3. **Sub-question 3:** Which meals best fit the user's available cooking time and dietary preferences?

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
